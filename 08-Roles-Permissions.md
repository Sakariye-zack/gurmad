# Roles & Permissions

| Role | Scope | Main responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Admin | Company-wide | Full visibility and system control |
| Management | Company-wide | Monitoring and reports |
| Zone Chairman/Gudoomiye | Assigned zone | Zone operations and supervision |
| Zone Accountant | Assigned zone | Zone financial visibility |
| Head of Cashiers | Assigned zone | Cashier supervision and cashout |
| Cashier | Assigned zone/customer portfolio | Collect customer payments |
| Collector | Assigned route/task | Waste collection only |
| Driver | Assigned truck/routes | Driving and route operations |
| Customer | Own account | Bills, payments, receipts and support |

## Mandatory isolation
Collector must not see invoice totals, balances, payment history, cashout or accounting information.
Cashier sees only assigned customer payment portfolios.
Head of Cashiers sees cashiers and cashout within assigned zone.
Zone Chairman sees assigned zone only.
Admin can see the whole company.
