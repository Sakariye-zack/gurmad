# Project Proposal
## Gurmad Waste Management — Smart Waste Management & Business Platform

### 1. Introduction
Gurmad Waste Management operates waste collection services in Togdheer. The proposed platform will digitize the complete company workflow from customer registration and zone operations to waste collection, payment collection, cashout, accounting, fleet management, HR, procurement, documents and management reporting.

### 2. Problem Statement
Manual processes can cause inaccurate records, missed houses, weak collection monitoring, payment leakage, difficult debt tracking, poor route planning, unclear staff accountability and slow financial reporting.

### 3. Objectives
- Digitize company operations.
- Improve waste collection.
- Control payments and cash.
- Track debts and partial payments.
- Manage zones, trucks and staff.
- Monitor live operations.
- Produce reliable financial statements.
- Create professional company documents.
- Provide customer and staff mobile access.

### 4. Scope
Customers, houses, zones, trucks, drivers, collectors, cashiers, Head of Cashiers, Zone Chairmen, tasks, routes, GPS, invoices, payments, debts, cashout, accounting, expenses, payroll, HR, procurement, inventory, documents, complaints, notifications, reports and portals.

### 5. Expected outcome
A single online system that gives Admin/Management full visibility while enforcing role-based and zone-based access.
