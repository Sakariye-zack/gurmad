# UI/UX Redesign

## Brand
Keep the existing Gurmad logo and green/orange brand colors.

## Direction
Modern enterprise SaaS design with clean spacing, strong typography, consistent cards/tables/forms, responsive layouts and minimal clutter.

## Admin
Company-wide dashboard with financial, operations, fleet and customer KPIs.

## Cashier
Fast customer search, balance visibility and payment entry.

## Collector
Task-first design: route, houses, service status and GPS. Financial fields hidden.

## Head of Cashiers
Zone cashiers, collection totals, cashout batches, variance and approval.

## Map
Make map a primary operations tool with filters for zones, houses, trucks, routes and progress.

## Navigation
Keep current activeView state-based navigation. Refactor into a page registry and reusable components instead of adding React Router now.
