# Documents & Templates

## Branding
Every official document must display the approved Gurmad Waste Management logo, company name, contact details and consistent green/orange identity.

## Required documents
- Invoice
- Payment Receipt
- Quotation
- Cashout Form
- Cash Collection Sheet
- Cashout Approval Form
- Expense Form
- Expense Claim
- Purchase Request
- Purchase Order
- Customer Service Agreement
- Employee Contract
- Vehicle Assignment Form
- Maintenance Report
- Collection Task Sheet
- Daily Collection Report
- Zone Report
- Financial Report

## Document lifecycle
Create → Preview → Generate PDF → Download/Print → Sign → Upload signed copy → Approve → Archive.

## Document metadata
Document number, date, creator, approver, status, version and related entity.
