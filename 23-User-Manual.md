# User Manual

## Admin
Manage company settings, users, roles, zones, customers, houses, trucks, staff, assignments, finance, documents, reports and live operations.

## Management
Monitor company KPIs, financial statements, operations, fleet and reports.

## Zone Chairman
Manage and monitor assigned zone only.

## Zone Accountant
View assigned-zone financial records and reports according to permissions.

## Head of Cashiers
Review cashiers, collections, cashout batches, variances and approvals within assigned zone.

## Cashier
Open assigned customers, review invoice/balance, collect Cash/ZAAD/eDahab, record payments and submit cashout.

## Collector
Open assigned task, navigate route, service houses, mark serviced/missed and report issues. No money information.

## Driver
View assigned truck and route, support transport and update vehicle/task status.

## Customer
View invoices, payments, receipts, balance and support.
