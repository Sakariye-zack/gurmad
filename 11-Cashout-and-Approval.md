# Cashout & Approval Workflow

## Chain
Cashier → Head of Cashiers → Accountant → Management

## Cashier
Collects payments, reviews own collection portfolio and submits collection batch.

## Head of Cashiers
Zone-scoped. Reviews cashier totals, verifies cash and digital transactions, records variances and approves/rejects cashout.

## Cashout form
The system generates a branded cashout form containing:
- Company logo/name
- Zone
- Cashier
- Period
- Customer count
- Cash
- ZAAD
- eDahab
- SLSH
- USD
- Total
- Variance
- Prepared by
- Approved by
- Signature fields

## Lifecycle
Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Approved/Rejected → Signed → Uploaded → Archived.

## Controls
Approved cashout cannot be silently edited. Adjustments require reason and audit trail.
