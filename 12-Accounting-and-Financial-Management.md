# Accounting & Financial Management

## Objective
Provide full company accounting rather than only income/expense reporting.

## Chart of Accounts
Support assets, liabilities, equity, revenue and expenses.

## General Ledger
All approved financial transactions post to the ledger through controlled journal entries.

## Accounts Receivable
Track invoices, customer balances, partial payments, overdue debt and collection history.

## Accounts Payable
Track supplier bills and company obligations.

## Cash/Bank
Track cash accounts and bank accounts where used.

## Revenue
Waste collection services, monthly invoices, one-time services, debt recovery and other income.

## Expenses
Fuel, maintenance, salaries, rent, utilities, procurement, equipment and other operating expenses.

## Statements
- Trial Balance
- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Accounts Receivable
- Accounts Payable

## Cash Flow
Opening balance + inflows − outflows = closing balance.

## Periods
Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, six-month, yearly and custom date range.

## Currency
SLSH and USD with historical exchange-rate preservation.
