# Mobile & Portal Requirements

## Admin Portal
Company-wide visibility for Admin and permitted management roles.

## Staff Portal
### Cashier
Assigned customers, balances, invoices, payment collection, payment history, cashout, receipts and notifications.

### Collector
Today's tasks, assigned houses, route, service status and issue reporting. No financial information.

### Driver
Truck, route, navigation and vehicle status.

## Customer Portal
Home, invoices, payments, receipts, notifications, support and account.

## PWA
Staff and Customer portals should remain mobile-first and installable where practical.
