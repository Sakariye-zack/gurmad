# Collector Task & Batch Workflow

## Principle
Collectors perform waste collection only. They do not collect or view customer money.

## Zone capacity
A zone can have 7 or more collectors.

## Batch example
Zone has 600 customers. A truck can serve 300 houses on a route.
- Batch 1: 300 houses
- Batch 2: remaining 300 houses

If truck capacity is lower, split into smaller batches.

## Assignment
Admin/authorized manager selects zone → truck → driver → collector(s) → houses/customers → route → date.

## History
Before creating a task, the system shows:
- Already serviced
- Due for collection
- Not yet serviced
- Missed
- Rescheduled

## Collector app
Shows assigned houses, route, collection status and issue reporting only.

## Status
Task: Pending, In Progress, Partially Completed, Completed, Cancelled.
House: Not Serviced, Serviced, Missed, Skipped, Rescheduled.
