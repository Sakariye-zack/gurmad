# 49. Notification & SMS Message Templates

## Invoice
Dear {customer_name}, your Gurmad invoice {invoice_number} is {amount} {currency}. Balance: {balance}. Thank you.

## Payment
Payment received from {customer_name}: {amount} {currency}, method {method}. Receipt: {receipt_number}.

## Debt
Dear {customer_name}, your Gurmad outstanding balance is {balance} {currency}. Please arrange payment.

## Collection
Gurmad collection service is scheduled for {date}. Please ensure access to the house.

## Task
Task {task_number} has been assigned to you for {zone} on {date}.

## Cashout
Cashout {cashout_number} is {status}.

## Complaint
Your complaint {complaint_number} is now {status}.

Templates must be approved by management and localized if Somali/English versions are required.
