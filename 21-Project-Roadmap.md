# Project Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Full Audit
Audit current 34 frontend components, 193 endpoints, 43 tables, activeView navigation, roles, permissions and workflows.

## Phase 2 — Business Logic
Correct zone scope, multiple collectors, batch collection, collection history, multiple cashiers, customer portfolios, debt and partial payment.

## Phase 3 — Accounting
Chart of Accounts, General Ledger, AR/AP, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and financial periods.

## Phase 4 — Documents
Branded invoice, receipt, quotation, cashout forms, approval, signing, download, upload and archive.

## Phase 5 — UI/UX
Redesign existing pages while preserving Gurmad branding and current architecture.

## Phase 6 — Map/GPS
Improve zones, houses, live trucks, routes and collection progress.

## Phase 7 — Mobile
Optimize Staff and Customer PWA workflows.

## Phase 8 — QA/Production
Security testing, financial testing, UAT, deployment, monitoring and backup validation.
