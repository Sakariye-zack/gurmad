# Testing & QA

## Unit
Invoice totals, balances, exchange rates, permissions and cashout calculations.

## Integration
API/database, payment callbacks, SMS, documents, GPS and Socket.IO.

## Role tests
Admin full visibility; zone roles restricted; cashier portfolio restricted; collector financial isolation.

## Financial tests
Full payment, partial payment, debt, duplicate payment prevention, cashout variance, currency conversion, journal posting and financial statements.

## Operations tests
Zone, truck, staff assignments, batch tasks, collection history, missed houses, GPS and route history.

## UAT
Admin, Management, Zone Chairman, Zone Accountant, Head of Cashiers, Cashier, Collector, Driver and Customer.

## Release gate
No critical security or financial defects.
