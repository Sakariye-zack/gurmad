# Cashier Collection & Debt

## Principle
Cashier collects money from customers serviced by the operation. Cashier does not perform waste collection.

## Multiple cashiers
One zone can have 7 or more cashiers. Customer portfolios are assigned so each cashier knows who they must collect from.

## Cashier view
- Assigned customers
- Invoice total
- Paid amount
- Outstanding balance
- Payment history
- Paid/Partial/Unpaid/Overdue status

## Partial payment
Invoice = $20. Customer pays $10.
Paid = $10, Balance = $10, Status = Partial.
Later payments reduce the balance.

## Payment methods
Cash, ZAAD, eDahab.

## Currency
SLSH and USD.

## Debt collection
Cashier has a dedicated “Customers Who Owe” list for assigned customers.
