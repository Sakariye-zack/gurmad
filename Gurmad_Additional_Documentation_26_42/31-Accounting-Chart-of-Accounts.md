# 31. Accounting & Chart of Accounts

## Suggested account structure
### Assets
1000 Cash
1010 Bank
1020 Accounts Receivable
1030 Inventory
1040 Vehicles
1050 Equipment

### Liabilities
2000 Accounts Payable
2010 Accrued Expenses
2020 Other Liabilities

### Equity
3000 Owner/Share Capital
3100 Retained Earnings

### Revenue
4000 Waste Collection Revenue
4010 One-Time Service Revenue
4020 Other Operating Revenue

### Expenses
5000 Fuel
5010 Vehicle Maintenance
5020 Salaries & Wages
5030 Rent
5040 Utilities
5050 Communications
5060 Procurement
5070 Equipment
5080 Other Operating Expenses

Account codes can be adjusted to the company's accountant-approved structure.

## Posting principle
Every approved accounting transaction must balance Debit = Credit.
