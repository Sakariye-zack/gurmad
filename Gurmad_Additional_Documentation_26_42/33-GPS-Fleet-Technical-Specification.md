# 33. GPS & Fleet Technical Specification

## Truck data
Plate, model, capacity, status, assigned zone, driver, collector(s), GPS device.

## GPS
Store latitude, longitude, timestamp, speed where available, heading where available and device status.

## Monitoring
Admin sees live trucks, current route, completed houses, remaining houses and last update.

## Alerts
- GPS offline
- Route deviation
- Long stop
- Capacity/task completion
- Maintenance due

## Fleet
Fuel logs, maintenance, inspections, vehicle documents and assignment history.

## Privacy
Location tracking must be limited to company vehicles and authorized operational use.
