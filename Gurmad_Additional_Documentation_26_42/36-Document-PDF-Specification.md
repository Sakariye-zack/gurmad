# 36. Document & PDF Specification

## Common header
Gurmad Waste Management logo, company name, contact details, document title and document number.

## Invoice
Customer, house, zone, service, invoice date, due date, amount, currency, balance, payment instructions.

## Receipt
Receipt number, customer, invoice, amount paid, method, currency, reference, date and cashier.

## Quotation
Quotation number, customer, service description, quantities, price, currency, validity, terms and approval.

## Cashout
Cashier, zone, period, customer count, Cash/ZAAD/eDahab totals, currencies, expected/actual, variance, signatures.

## Requirements
Print-ready PDF, download, document version, signature fields, signed upload and archive.
