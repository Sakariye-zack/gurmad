# 32. Financial Control & Reconciliation SOP

## Daily cashier closing
1. Stop daily collection.
2. Review all invoices/payments.
3. Separate Cash, ZAAD and eDahab.
4. Compare expected vs actual.
5. Record variance.
6. Submit cashout.

## Head of Cashiers
Verify each cashier in the assigned zone, review supporting records, approve/reject and sign.

## Daily financial closing
Payments + expenses + approved adjustments → accounting review → daily report.

## Month-end
Reconcile receivables, payables, cash, digital payments, expenses and ledger balances.

## Year-end
Close fiscal period only after management/accounting review and backup.

## Control principle
No person should create, approve and reconcile the same high-risk transaction without appropriate authorization.
