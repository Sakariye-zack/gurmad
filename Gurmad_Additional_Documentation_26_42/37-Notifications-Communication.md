# 37. Notifications & Communication

## Channels
In-app, SMS and WhatsApp where enabled.

## Customer
Invoice created, payment confirmed, receipt, debt reminder, service reminder, complaint update.

## Staff
Task assignment, route update, collection changes, cashout status, approval/rejection.

## Admin
Payment failures, cashout variance, overdue debt, GPS offline, maintenance alerts and critical system alerts.

## Rules
Do not expose sensitive data in notification previews unnecessarily. Log notification status and retry failures safely.
